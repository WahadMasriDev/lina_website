02 About

I’m Lina — a graphic & digital designer growing into artistic direction. I shape brands as complete worlds: identity, editorial systems, digital surfaces, and the experimental 3D imagery that gives them attitude.

My approach is multicultural by nature — Egyptian, raised between countries, and quadrilingual (Arabic, French, English, Spanish). Curiosity carries every project to its edge: I value teamwork but work just as comfortably on my own, always looking for the cleanest solution to the messiest problem.
