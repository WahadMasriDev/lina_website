PHOTOS — drop your portrait here
================================

The About section shows a soft chrome gradient until you add your photo.
Add this exact filename and it appears automatically:

  lina-portrait.jpg   -> portrait in the About section

Recommended
-----------
  Portrait orientation (4:5), ~1200 x 1500 px, < 400 KB (JPG quality ~80).
  Lowercase name, no spaces.

You can reuse the photo from your CV. Only use images you own.
